2 Dig

A surface problem is often just a symptom. To understand the root cause, you need to dig deeper. This is the Five Whys technique, which comes from Toyota's manufacturing process.

Example from a different business — not generated from your answers:

"Independent fashion brands struggle to produce and sell clothing sustainably while keeping prices affordable for everyday consumers."

  1. Sustainable materials and ethical suppliers cost significantly more than fast-fashion alternatives.
  2. Small brands can't get the bulk-order discounts that large fashion retailers use to offset that cost.
  3. They don't have the order volume, because they're small and reaching a limited customer base.
  4. Customers who care about sustainability still compare prices against cheap fast fashion and hesitate.
  5. There's no accepted price norm yet for "affordable sustainable" clothing, so every brand is guessing alone.
